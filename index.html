ROLL BUTTONS
On, the LR and RR buttons roll the ship: hold one for the knife edge, tap it twice for a barrel roll that way. Off, the old gesture instead -- double-tap the screen on the side you want, and hold the second tap to stay on edge.
PAINT TO LOCK
On, a target locks only while the crosshair is actually on it. Off, anything within a quarter of the screen paints, which plays as an auto-lock.
